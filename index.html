How I build.

The same three steps, whether it's a lakehouse or a team of AI agents.

  1. 1

    Model the data first.

    Medallion layers, clear schemas and validation at every hop, so analysts and models read from one trusted source.

  2. 2

    Engineer for failure.

    Checkpoints, retries, backfills, SLAs and outboxes. Pipelines should recover on their own, not page someone at 3 a.m.

  3. 3

    Put AI to work, with guardrails.

    Agents get tight tool budgets, human approval where it matters, evals, and a cost meter from day one.
